V133: independent read-only closure of saved V132 sequential models.

V132 jobs are not retried. Each produced its complete packed model, code metadata,
construction report and per-stage diagnostics before failing to serialize a bytes
execution-provenance field in its final completion receipt. Original FAILED 1:0
states and absent completion receipts remain unchanged. V133 is a separate audit,
not a successful replacement construction or independent recalibration.

Admission requires actual FAILED 1:0 accounting, the exact final serialization
trace, all required files and the completed 331-check qualification. It binds and
rehashes the entire producer source package, original checkpoints, target, token
records, original fixed model, stage artifacts and final model. It checks every
packed stage hash/grid, retained membership, target identity, first-stage equality,
the actual installed predecessor hash chain, all point-token admissions, complete
per-code interval decision counts, unchanged caps and no exact-rational decisions.
Eight generated corruption/serialization checks run in each audit worker. Tests
are software evidence; the saved model/stage artifacts supply empirical evidence.

The original native runtime sidecar was not saved. Its constructor was source-bound
and ran the frozen pinned container launcher, but V133 does not invent the missing
worker process totals or runtime sidecar. Charge actual Slurm TotalCPU for original
failed jobs and separate audit CPU. No old model or receipt is changed. Audit receipt
marks this provenance limitation and preserves producer job status explicitly.

Four one-CPU, 16 GiB audits, at most 300 CPU seconds and 600 wall seconds each;
128 MiB individual output bound. Reserve 4x300 + 4x60 launch = 1440 CPU seconds,
within unused original sequential campaign reserve after actual predecessor costs.
No inference, calibration, solver call, source feature extraction or corpus access.
A later separately registered quality evaluation may admit the audited immutable
model bytes through this completed audit, preserving the failed producer status.
