Corrections incorporated
------------------------
1. The abstract now describes additive record summaries defined by independent
   references. Their sums depend on the calibration records; calling the summed
   statistics corpus-independent would have been inaccurate.

2. The finite response bound now requires the proved region to contain both the
   full chart segment and, when present, the residual segment. It specifies the
   Hessian norm convention and residual derivative control along the entire path.
   Automatic affine implementation still requires zero residual. This preserves
   the distinction between ideal derivatives and certified finite-program error.

3. The algorithm explicitly obtains a new unresolved group's replay after each
   failed retry. Successful exactness and eventual completion are separate:
   the evaluator is partial, and required successful finite calls, arithmetic
   termination within resources, finite groups/stages and replay progress are
   additional completion hypotheses. Abort is never an approximate commitment.

4. Lifetime notation now distinguishes complete request costs F_h and R_h from
   the earlier avoidable-work term F. Missing/failed horizons remain undefined,
   with observed costs and unknown-cost indicators retained. Lost observations
   cannot imply that all consumed work has been recovered.

5. The draft identifies lifetime as the sum of attributable system transactions,
   not elapsed research-harness time. Research oracle/equality/lineage and other
   orchestration work are excluded symmetrically. Available observations remain
   separate and unmeasured components unknown; no complete harness wall-time or
   CPU accounting is claimed. Required next-step state validation uses actual
   prior state-producing child receipts and model/state artifacts, not research
   comparison copies, compact lineage records or comparator/observer archives.

6. Clock exclusions now include observer bootstrap and measurement-manifest
   preparation before the declared interval, as well as final observer reporting
   receipt/snapshot writes afterward. Required child/controller commits and
   cleanup inside the declared interval remain charged. This is a declared
   nonrecursive transaction boundary, not an all-software/all-hardware clock.

7. Evidence-slot partition requirements now distinguish document/record
   disjointness from duplicate-content/token-sequence overlap checks. Ordinary
   token vocabulary IDs need not be disjoint across corpora.

8. The resource bridge description reflects completed trusted protocol-ledger
   and supplied-root file snapshots. Scientific provenance remains independently
   unestablished; missing resource/diagnostic evidence remains explicit. These
   snapshots do not establish global/project CPU use or physical disk allocation.

Mathematical checks and interpretation limits
--------------------------------------------
- Compact enclosure: the omitted P is positive semidefinite and bounded by its
  trace beta times I; adding beta I gives a positive-semidefinite surrogate.
  The asymmetric Gram error budgets, fixed ridge and positive alpha premise
  support Proposition 1. Replay tightens budgets at a fixed prefix, but candidate
  margins and successful acceptance need not improve monotonically.
- Full quadratic stores the Gram polynomial of an affine feature response.
  It is not a second-order Taylor transformer. Compact rational-slot savings
  are separated from bits, bytes, temporary memory, metadata and directions.
  Full-polynomial query lower bounds are not asserted as necessary storage for
  every exact discrete answer.
- The forced-prefix displacement lemma uses positive relative endpoints and
  the same preceding code prefix. Classical matrix geometry and the sharp
  abstract constant are attributed; no novelty is assigned to that constant
  or to the GPTQ/nearest-plane connection.
- Stage induction uses the newly certified ancestor prefix, followed by
  topological induction. Intrinsic exact moments and canonical serialization
  yield retained-state equality within a fixed tier. Trusted origin, checked
  deleted payloads, record locality and retained replay access are substantive
  assumptions. External copies and pretrained-weight information are excluded.
- The no-replay corollary is implementation-matched and conditional on every
  stage's actual repaired prefix, normalized positive-energy margins, domain,
  finite-error and zero-energy tie premises. Small deletion size alone does not
  imply it. The stronger ideal whitened theorem is not attributed to the actual
  quadratic verifier without its missing premises/implementation.
- The complete-cost ratio is an arithmetic consequence of explicitly bounded
  excess work and demonstrated comparable work. Acceptance fractions do not
  supply cost-weighted avoided-work fractions. Rational operation counts do
  not imply constant-time word operations or practical speed.
- The lifetime identity charges each system's own original output once and
  every required request. A first break-even crossing can reverse. A finite
  favorable identity provides neither population reliability nor universal
  full-model improvement.

Comparator and evidence checks
------------------------------
Ordinary model-only fresh constructs the complete retained model without the
response index. Direct full-state fresh constructs both model and canonical
state. Equally indexed fresh receives the same information and solver as repair.
All four require identical target/stage codes; state equality applies only to
matching state-producing interfaces. No deletion-exclusive solver benefit follows
from an indexed shared solver or from beating the more expensive state builder.

The ordered study has distinct original model-only preparation, fixed original
normalization, verified entering state, and cumulative versus newly deleted IDs.
Shared indexed preparation is counted once per system with explicit correlation.
Oracle and quality costs cannot be added asymmetrically to a comparator. Reused
observations cannot count as fresh repetitions; failed/unstarted slots stay in
the planned denominator, and an incomplete lifetime stays null.

Evidence slots have explicit artifacts, verifier/analysis paths, denominator and
falsification requirements. Changed-prefix coverage concerns all nonempty groups
with actually changed transitive ancestors and actual target-feature work;
unknown or capped diagnostics cannot imply zero work. Absolute NLP quality is
separate from retained-oracle equality. No software fixture, invented curve,
planning model size or prospective manifest may populate an empirical slot.
