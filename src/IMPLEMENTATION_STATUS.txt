- response_service_adapter.py: original per-record descriptor bridge.
- aggregate_response_service.py: complete compact group state and repair.
- transformer_backend.py: pinned library-math scalar decoder and identity gate.
- certified_intervals.py: rational enclosures and certified nonlinear rounding.
- certified_transformer.py: automatic interval jets and finite-error proofs.
- checkpoint_adapter.py: strict local GPT-2 safetensors loading.
- work_scheduler.py: separate cooperative bounded-work fallback utility.

V_cert is a separate finite decoder target.
It does not silently replace legacy library-math V or floating quantizer E.
Its automatic provider checks a fixed corpus-independent affine chart.
Unknown proof conditions cause retained replay.
A finite-evaluator failure aborts the request without committing a model.

The compact service stores group matrices and O(NL) record metadata.
It regenerates and checks deleted-record contributions before subtraction.
It retains no individual matrix descriptors or data-dependent primitive cache.
Logical state equals fresh retained construction after repeated deletion.
Metadata scans, source reads, preparation, and integer sizes remain costs.

See docs/VALIDATION.md for final correctness tests and source hashes.
These checks are not research benchmark or synthetic dataset results.
