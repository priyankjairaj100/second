Prospective interpretation of the deep speed/transfer campaign

The scientific claim is not selected by which result looks most favorable.
Preserve all cells, endpoints, failures and earlier negative findings.

V80 asks whether the optimizations change complete requests, and whether
compressed storage beats the equally optimized modern lossless control on the
existing root. Eight actual successor comparisons to independent reconstruction,
both audits and terminal host accounting are necessary for usable evidence.
One observation in each cell is an engineering screen, not stable latency.
Compare reference->optimized within each representation for attribution; compare
optimized compressed->optimized modern for the practical storage choice. Native
setup, context loading, validation and cleanup must remain in the primary clock.
Use the same measured clock for every ratio. Do not sum nested phase timings.

A component gain can be large while its total contribution is small. After V80,
use actual non-overlapping request phase measurements to explain this. Common
solve/context/source/encoding cost, bounded-cache overhead and improved controls
may limit the benefit of faster compressed decoding. A resource refusal is not
proof that transfer is scientifically impossible; an execution or protocol bug
requires a separate new registration, never a silent rerun of consumed attempts.

V81 tests the format/guards for a second complete depth. V82 is input/actual-target
admission. Neither establishes transfer. A subsequent matched experiment must
use the same4x128-token workload and approved grids for both DistilGPT2 and GPT2,
fresh retained-teacher oracles for each checkpoint, all24/48 stages, full model,
state and trust bytes, terminal accounting and every registered outcome. Model
bytes across the two checkpoints need not match. This is related-checkpoint/depth
transfer; two GPT2-family models do not establish cross-architecture generality.
The small workload and changed512 normalization must be disclosed. Quality under
V72's different target is not evidence for the new target's quality.

If V80 and the matched transfer screen are correct and useful, a separate timing
registration should compare the two optimized representations using eight paired
blocks per checkpoint/endpoint, with seeded randomized within-pair order and
all64 requests declared in advance. Independent fresh processes/contexts and
same audited predecessors/oracles are required. Exact budgets must be calculated
from the admitted target and prior CPU observations before registration; if the
preserved ceiling cannot admit all64 requests, narrow the scientific question or
retain descriptive single-observation results. Do not spend leftover budget on
favorable endpoints or add repetitions based on p-values. This is a design
proposal, not an approved registered experiment or permission for larger caps.

For that proposed study the primary outcome is complete controller transaction
wall time; worker/request clocks, CPU, peak memory and live/checkpoint-inclusive
storage are secondary outcomes. Publish every pair and each of the four strata.
Use a predeclared one-sided paired assignment/randomization test of faster
compressed transactions with Holm correction across the four strata; also report
paired ratios and uncertainty. No pooled average should conceal a losing stratum.
No claim of universal speed follows even if all four strata support improvement.
Cache/host conditions, pinned implementations, source counts and scope remain
part of the result. Any failed trial is reported and prevents an all-trials
success statement; do not quietly analyze only completed favorable pairs.

Writing decision after evidence closure:
- Correct, reliably faster requests on both tested checkpoints: lead with exact
  compressed repair that improves the measured storage/time frontier, state the
  tested models/workload and retain all original comparisons.
- Correct with a localized gain or a loss against the improved lossless control:
  lead with certified repair and the measured storage/access/latency tradeoff;
  present the optimization attribution and restricted operating conditions.
- Correct but no practical speed gain across the tested settings: retain the
  bounded tradeoff contribution, explain measured cost attribution and negative
  transfer/speed evidence precisely. Do not claim a theorem against all speedups.
- Incorrect or unaudited new execution: keep the existing verified V72/V74 story;
  preserve the failure and withhold new scientific claims.

Generality is helpful for practical importance, but it is not logically required
for the established conditional exactness and bounded storage/access result.
Its absence reduces external validity and makes the magnitude of demonstrated
savings, realistic deployment assumptions and clear comparison costs more
important to reviewers. A correct narrower result can be written honestly; it
must not be sold as general acceleration. Broad language requires actual diverse
workload/checkpoint evidence, not only generic mathematical notation or software
support for more stages. Conference acceptance is not guaranteed by either path.
