\section*{31. A ridge-aware certificate portfolio}
Revision 7 supplements the spectral verifier with exact interval reverse-LDL.
The optional policy runs the spectral test first.
It returns immediately after spectral acceptance.
Otherwise it checks the same candidate against entrywise covariance bounds before replay.
This preserves accepted spectral decisions at the same proposal.
It does not guarantee lower runtime or completion under finite resource limits.

Fix the current certified prefix and retained records.
Their normalized covariance satisfies
\[
 H^*=\lambda I+M_0^{-1}\sum_{j\in R}X_jX_j^\top\succeq\lambda I.
\]
Suppose intrinsic moments prove $-aI\preceq H^*-S\preceq bI$, with $a,b\ge0$.
Shifting the error by $(b-a)I/2$ gives norm at most $(a+b)/2$.
Therefore a sound entry enclosure is
\[
 H^*_{ii}\in[S_{ii}-a,S_{ii}+b],\qquad
 H^*_{ij}\in[S_{ij}-(a+b)/2,S_{ij}+(a+b)/2]\quad(i\ne j).
\]
Diagonal bounds retain their asymmetry.
The off-diagonal radius is sharp under these premises.
Normalize raw group uncertainty exactly once and include the ridge exactly once.

\begin{lemma}[A common ridge survives elimination]
If $H\succeq\lambda I$, every Schur complement and every reverse-LDL pivot has lower bound $\lambda$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Partition $H$ into blocks $A,B,C$.
For its Schur complement $T=A-BC^{-1}B^\top$,
\[
 x^\top Tx=\min_y (x,y)^\top H(x,y)
 \ge\lambda\norm x_2^2.
\]
Apply this identity at every elimination step.
\end{proof}

Initialize rational entry intervals from the covariance enclosure.
Process coordinates in reverse order:
\[
 [t_k]=[A_{kk}]\cap[\lambda,\infty),\qquad
 [L_{ki}]=[A_{ki}]/[t_k],\qquad
 [A_{ij}]\leftarrow[A_{ij}]-[A_{ki}][A_{kj}]/[t_k].
\]
Use exact interval squares for diagonal updates.
An empty pivot intersection signals invalid premises; it cannot produce acceptance.
The actual covariance satisfies the ridge even when other matrices inside the rectangular enclosure are indefinite.
Induction through these interval operations encloses its exact factors.

For each fixed candidate row $q$, enclose
\[
 [v_i]=w_i+\sum_{h<i}[L_{ih}](w_h-q_h).
\]
Lower-tie rounding cells are $(m_{\rm lower},m_{\rm upper}]$.
Accept only when $\inf[v_i]>m_{\rm lower}$ and $\sup[v_i]\le m_{\rm upper}$ at finite endpoints.
Coordinate induction establishes every emitted code.
Stage induction then establishes the complete sequential target.
Interval dependency and integer growth can still cause expensive or unsuccessful verification.

\section*{32. Strict certificate improvement and its cost}
\begin{theorem}[Intersection preserves fixed-candidate acceptance]
Let $\mathcal J\subseteq\mathcal I$ be nonempty sound entry enclosures of the same covariance.
Fix weights, candidate, ridge, grids, and interval operation order.
Assume both calculations complete.
Every factor and decision interval from $\mathcal J$ lies inside its counterpart from $\mathcal I$.
Thus acceptance from $\mathcal I$ implies acceptance from $\mathcal J$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Interval addition, subtraction, multiplication, squaring, and positive division preserve inclusion.
Intersection with the same ridge half-line also preserves inclusion.
Induct through elimination and the fixed-candidate decision expressions.
Containment inside the same rounding cells follows.
\end{proof}

For several valid boxes with identical target, prefix, retained-set, and normalization bindings, intersect their entries.
Empty intersections cannot be treated as vacuous success.
Different candidates do not inherit this theorem automatically.

\note{Canonical bank design, when needed.}

\note{Full quadratic response tier.}
The service now stores every oriented cross-moment of its affine feature response.
For $Z(a)=Z_0+\sum_{t=1}^r a_tZ_t$, it reconstructs $Z(a)Z(a)^\top$ exactly.
Negative coefficients retain their signs and cross-moment orientation.
The finite feature error supplies the existing unwhitened Gram error bound.
The stronger whitened theorem remains a separate, unimplemented acceptance rule.

Per group, quadratic response and scalar error storage use
\[
 \frac{(r+1)(r+2)}2d^2+\frac{(r+3)(r+4)}2

rational slots.
The compact linear tier remains available.
Separate schemas bind quadratic state and its index.
Both support exact subtraction, bounded loading, indexed fresh solving, and empty retained sets.
Preparation, extraction products, stored slots, integer sizes, and serialization remain explicit costs.

\note{Original-model invariant-feature baseline.}
A separate canonical cache stores true sequential Grams under its current quantized model.
Its state includes the model, retained source bindings, and each stage's ancestor-prefix identity.
For a request, process stages in dependency order.
Compare every new transitive ancestor with its old installed code matrix.
When they agree, evaluate deleted contributions under that prefix and subtract them from the cached Gram.
When they differ, evaluate retained records under the new prefix and rebuild that stage's Gram.
Then quantize and store the exact refreshed Gram.

\begin{theorem}[Canonical identity-cache baseline]
Under the declared deterministic target and successful required evaluations, this procedure returns fresh retained model and cache state.
It remains valid after repeated, reordered, empty, and complete deletions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Identical finite ancestors give identical record-local features, so exact subtraction yields the retained Gram.
Changed ancestors instead receive an exact retained reconstruction.
Coordinate quantization and stage induction establish each new output and its true Gram.
Canonical source bindings and serialization depend only on retained records and the fixed target.
Induct over requests.
\end{proof}

This cache has a separate auxiliary-state interface from intrinsic response summaries.
It is an honest baseline, not a claim of identical storage requirements.
Indexed fresh receives the same valid cache and old model.
Its solver is identical to repair's solver.
Validation-only index preparation defers prefix-dependent deleted extraction to the solver.
Count every such operation in its actual scope.

\note{Sequence execution.}

\section*{34. Execution accounting and remaining evidence}
Revision 7 separates software preparation from empirical evidence.
The protocol remains paused.
No model, tokenizer, calibration corpus, or research benchmark was downloaded or executed.
Correctness fixtures do not establish practical speed, useful coverage, or NLP quality.

\note{Four-process comparison.}
One limited setup process prepares and persists original canonical state, then exits.
Separate workers run repair, indexed fresh, and direct fresh in a declared order.
Every worker reloads its inputs and constructs its own service.
Repair and indexed fresh receive the same original state.
Direct fresh constructs retained output without reading the unused original index.
The parent compares every complete model and canonical state against direct fresh.
Missing or failed reference output prevents a successful exactness claim.

Each worker's measured boundary includes startup, input loading, service work, artifact synchronization, child receipt commitment, and process exit.
It excludes parent validation, equality checks, final parent commitment, and the enclosing worker-control receipt commitment.
Setup has a separate measured cost.
These are explicit worker boundaries, not a complete parent-orchestrated transaction.
Operating-system caches remain uncontrolled.
Warm comparisons remain available as separately labelled diagnostic runs.
Independent process peaks and final serialized integer sizes supplement internal telemetry.
Comprehensive transient arithmetic growth and full parent timing remain open.

\note{Durable CPU admission.}
A locked protocol-scoped ledger reserves each worker's allowance before launch.
Setup and method workers share that ledger across output directories.
Unknown or interrupted attempts retain their reservation.
Completed outcomes debit upward-rounded observed child CPU.
An overrun remains fully charged and blocks subsequent admission when the cap is exhausted.
Retries require a new reservation; verified completed resumes do not.
This is an admission contract, not absolute physical CPU containment.
Controller CPU, hostile descendant trees, and separate protocol scopes need additional accounting for a global project cap.

\note{Restart integrity.}
Restart verifies immutable worker receipts and successful or failed child artifacts.
A child may commit before its enclosing timing receipt becomes durable.
Such output cannot receive a new short successful timing through cached reuse.
The executor records failure instead.
This avoids substituting cached-restart latency for the original computation.
No trust claim extends to hostile storage or physical erasure.

\note{Open empirical gate.}
Standalone sequences, complete deletion, full quadratic responses, and original-model caching are implemented.
Bulk sequence admission and isolated confirmation inventory remain integration tasks.
A feasible real checkpoint route, actual record manifests, source partitions, precision justification, and quality evaluation remain unresolved.
The scalar reference target remains distinct from native deployment kernels.
The stronger whitened response bound and neural multi-domain bank remain optional extensions.

The next scientific decision is whether real retained calibration work can be avoided at an acceptable total cost.
Every accepted certificate must preserve the complete target and canonical live state.
Every comparison must charge its actual information, preparation, maintenance, and failure paths.
Theorems characterize sufficient conditions; only authorized real experiments can establish their practical frequency and value.
