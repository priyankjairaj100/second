The direct-Gram stage admits at most six billion structural work units and 512 MiB of explicit arrays.
Its structural assessment reports 1,812,013,056 work units and 333,250,560 explicit array bytes.
Its unchanged source-accumulation bounds remain separately enforced.

The separately registered phase allowance is 900 CPU seconds.
Existing ledgers remain immutable and retain their full previous charges.
These admission envelopes do not promise completion or actual peak RSS.

Every output row must pass the unchanged exact certificate.
Any unresolved row must leave its entire arm without accepted codes.
The policy continues separately defined comparator arms after a narrowly classified scientific refusal.
Native row status three denotes an unresolved cell; coefficient status two denotes numerical enclosure refusal.
The token solver's exact-fallback budget refusal also qualifies under its complete fixed message pattern.
Native runtime or allocation statuses do not qualify.
Infrastructure failures and unclassified exceptions stop execution.
The worker does not commit approximate codes or retry selected rows under weaker conditions.
There is one attempt, without automatic retry or sample replacement.

Each arm records either certification or scientific refusal.
Refusal preserves the error type, exact message, and incomplete attempt time.
No reconstruction speed ratio is defined for refused arms.
