Second TACL final rewrite, 28 October 2026

Central contribution
Lossy calibration evidence can preserve exact adaptive model decisions. Recovering
a discrete low-bit model does not require recovering every original feature word.
This is the strongest story supported by the frozen work. It combines a fixed,
source-local calibration target, a uniform feature-box certificate, bounded exact
replay, and canonical state for successive deletions. It is not a new general
interval-verification principle or a general acceleration result.

Practical relevance
A calibration collection may lose duplicated, corrupted or disallowed records.
Maintenance then needs the defined retained-data model and a usable next state.
The relevant choice is which evidence to retain: exact features, interval-valued
features, or aggregate statistics backed by source access. Compare deployed bytes,
source traversals, complete latency and preparation costs together. The strongest
lossless cache wins the final latency comparison; compression offers a modest
storage alternative. Its practical appeal is conditional on storage/access costs,
not an experimentally validated deployment demand curve.

Paper structure
Pages 1-2 explain the maintenance problem, discrete-information distinction and a
worked two-weight example in which deletion changes a code despite lossy evidence.
Page 3 defines the finite target, grids and source-local features before formal use.
Pages 4-6 derive coefficient and decision-margin bounds and give all three proofs.
The remaining main pages define controls and complete accounting, report every
comparison, distinguish the two calibration roots and quality population, relate
the work to prior art, and state implementation/reproduction and use conditions.
References begin on page 11. No appendix or outside review supplement is required
to understand the theory or principal results.

Scientific preservation
All prior experimental sources and the 14 evidence/source components are unchanged.
The first service study, optimization, matched two-checkpoint screen and untouched
quality study remain separate. The smaller matched targets have no quality result.
Single observations do not establish stable latency or cross-architecture transfer.
Failures and negative findings are retained in the author-side evidence archive;
material scope is attached directly to the relevant result or theorem.
No old registration, collector, numerical job or audit was executed again.

Changes beyond prose
The new margin calculation is an elementary consequence of the triangle inequality;
it explains the stored precision requirement and conservatism of interval products.
The token-space coefficient identity is derived from the same ridge system, not a
new measured implementation. The toy example is checked with exact fractions and
is explicitly not an empirical result. No new scientific claim comes from tests.

Venue and delivery
The official TACL submission style replaces ACL style. Long operational identifiers
and repeated caveats no longer drive the opening. The AI-use disclosure is retained
without an acknowledgments section. MEDU, QEBVerif and WikiText citations point to
published versions; model-card citations are pinned and separately rendered.
The native editor remains the working document. A workspace-local official Tectonic
export helper resolves the former PDF-export block without uploading manuscript
content, installing a system TeX distribution, or requiring the failed UI service.
The portable compiler release digest and exact style bytes are recorded.
Submission eligibility and personal author declarations remain author-controlled;
the scientific manuscript and submission-format artifacts are prepared, not uploaded.
