Measured explanation and remaining uncertainty
Both compressed GPT2 requests at three records replay the final-block MLP
up-projection and traverse 144 source-stage/record pairs each. Optimized compressed
stage work takes 1458.767 seconds, including 695.104 seconds of nested source
extraction, versus 476.578 seconds for optimized modern storage. Other 14 requests
have no source traversal or fallback. Even without replay, at DistilGPT2/two
records the optimized compressed stage work takes 267.560 versus 114.651 seconds;
its validation cost of 2.266 versus 9.120 seconds does not offset that difference.
These phase observations explain where measured costs accumulate, but do not
isolate decoder, cache, kernel or certification effects. Decoder-only duration is
not measured; source extraction is nested and must never be double-counted.

Each cell is one fixed-order fresh-process observation with uncontrolled OS cache,
one CPU and 16 GiB. Both checkpoints share a GPT-family lineage and the same small
exposed four-by-128-token root, fixed normalization 512 and 4->3->2 deletion.
The existing token solver was fixed prospectively for all point-valued solves in
every arm and oracle after the earlier refusal/stage diagnostic. Box/Gram policies,
the exact target and approved 1,327,104-entry grid limit remained unchanged.
These results refute an observed win at every tested setting, not all possible
future algorithms, hardware, workloads or timing distributions. Shared numerical
code also means fresh reconstruction is not an independent implementation.

Why stop additional speed experiments
The prospective V82 decision rules select the bounded tradeoff framing after a
localized win or loss. V89 already showed mixed optimized endpoint latency
(0.90% faster at twelve records; 12.73% slower at six), and its attributed
preparation-plus-two-request cost favored modern storage. The completed matched
screen now loses at all four tested strata. Repeating a selected favorable case
would be biased; a balanced 64-request study would estimate latency uncertainty
but has no evidence-based general-win hypothesis here. Spare budget is not a
scientific justification. A later new question requires a separate rationale and
registration; no current registered job may be retried or duplicated.

Importance to the paper
Broader speed and transfer would strengthen practical importance and external
validity, but are not prerequisites for conditional exactness and the measured
bounded tradeoff. Their absence materially limits the empirical contribution:
the demonstrated storage gains are modest, latency often worsens, source access
can be required, and the fixed-teacher target differs from sequential calibration.
The defensible contribution is the specified recovery contract, executable
certification/canonical state and an honest evaluation of operating limits.
Do not market this as a general acceleration method or imply acceptance prospects.
