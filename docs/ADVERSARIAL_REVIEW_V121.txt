ADVERSARIAL MANUSCRIPT REVIEW V121
28 October 2026
Title: Lossy Calibration Archives for Exact Model Repair
Scope: scientific argument, proofs, numerical claims, references and final PDF.
This is an internal adversarial review, not an external referee report.

VERDICT AND CENTRAL STORY
The defensible contribution is a certificate-and-state construction that turns
lossy calibration evidence into exactly specified discrete model repairs, together
with complete measurements of the storage, source-access and latency choices.
The useful conceptual distinction is between reconstructing exact feature values
and determining exact weight codes. A feature enclosure can settle the latter
without settling the former. Successful repairs also produce canonical state for
the next deletion. Changed codes are covered; this is not only an unchanged-model
stability argument.

This is a bounded contribution. The evidence does not support general acceleration,
cross-architecture transfer or improved inference efficiency. The current results
support a storage-led maintenance setting with an explicit tolerance for repair
latency and possible source replay. The rewrite presents that result directly.
Publication importance remains a judgment for reviewers; citation and correctness
checks alone cannot establish TACL-level significance.

RESOLVED FINDINGS

1. Target ambiguity and an over-broad GPTQ reading (Sections 2, 3 and 8).
The earlier description left too much room to interpret the target as ordinary
GPTQ or a global quantized optimum. The revised definition states the quadratic
proxy, conditional continuous minimization and subsequent discrete rounding.
The greedy procedure need not find the global discrete optimum. Fixed teacher,
positive ridge, fixed original normalization, and base-only dyadic grids are part
of the target. Published GPTQ uses asymmetric min-max grids and quantized-prefix
propagation. The quality comparator is now explicitly a matched sequential
constructor in this target family, not an off-the-shelf GPTQ implementation.

2. Incomplete attribution of known rounding theory (Sections 3 and 8).
QuIP and the GPTQ/Babai geometry analysis were absent. Both are added. The existing
quadratic/LDL rounding analysis and nearest-plane connection are credited. The
Babai distortion bound is identified with its no-clipping premise. That theorem
is not silently applied to the manuscript's finite saturated grids. The claimed
new construction concerns uncertain calibration evidence and reusable repair state.

3. Overlap with executable certification literature (Section 8).
ExecCert already turns residual bounds into discrete prediction agreement through
margins. The revised comparison acknowledges this explicitly. Interval bounds and
proposal/verification separation are established tools. The distinction is the
adaptive chain of weight-code decisions and its canonical successor state, rather
than a generic claim to have invented discrete-output certification.

4. Missing token-space derivation and proof premises (Sections 3-5).
The manuscript now includes both feature-space and token-space coefficient
identities, the token residual bound and its positive-definite inverse argument.
A numerical proposal stays fixed during a verification check. For uncertain
features, the inequalities must hold uniformly through outward enclosure; an
arbitrary entrywise Gram enclosure is not itself assumed positive definite.
The suffix identity, spectral bound, prefix induction, exact source subtraction
and canonical-state composition were manually checked against their premises.
Independent rational checks cover 315 small matrices, including rank-deficient
features with positive ridge, 459 coefficient/bound cases and 459 rounding-cell
boundary cases. They support the derivation review; they are not a formal proof
of the entire executable implementation or new empirical evidence.

5. Confusing sufficient certification with identifiability (Section 4.3).
Two realizations in one feature box that yield different codes establish genuine
ambiguity. Separate coefficient intervals may also refuse when all realizations
agree, because interval combinations can be unattainable. The distinction is now
explicit. A certificate refusal alone is not evidence of information impossibility.

6. Complexity language (Section 4.4).
The displayed work polynomials are admission proxies, not bounds on the entire
verified request. Interval width, decoding, repeated verification and replay also
affect runtime. The common token policy in the final matched study is distinguished
from the earlier dispatcher without claiming an asymptotic total-cost theorem.

7. Timing and source-access units (Section 6 and Tables 1-3).
The baseline request clock excludes caller context loading and controller overhead;
optimization and matched studies use complete host clocks. Comparisons remain
within each study. Source access counts source-stage traversals, not distinct disk
reads or network requests. Nested extraction time is not added to its parent stage
time. Optimized packages contain multiple changes, so their speed differences are
not attributed solely to a decoder or individual kernel.

8. Storage denominator and practical relevance (Abstract; Sections 7 and 9).
The abstract now includes the matched checkpoint-inclusive savings of 1.30-2.24%
alongside the 7.01-7.86% live-state savings. A deployed model still needs unchanged
checkpoint components. The target preserves binary64 feature values; comparison
against a conventional lower-precision activation cache would require its own
correctness contract. Repair-state savings imply neither reduced inference memory
nor faster serving.

9. Quality estimand and population (Sections 6.2 and 7.4).
The pooled ratio is defined from article mean negative log losses, using equal
127-prediction counts. Paired resampling keeps whole articles and uses the next
higher empirical quantile. The original 320-article bounded frame, guard thresholds,
teacher-selection exposure, 40-bit refusal and separate 48-bit study remain. The
quality result belongs to the earlier DistilGPT2 target with normalization 1664;
it does not validate the later 512-normalized matched targets. No quality gain is
claimed: the reported paired intervals lie above one.

10. Bibliographic error and metadata conflicts (References).
MEDU's Alon Helvitz was corrected to Alon Helvits. The primary PDF, publisher-
deposited Crossref record and author institution agree; the arXiv abstract metadata
uses the other spelling. Xiao-Wen Chang in ExecCert was retained after checking

names follow the primary proceedings PDF. Model cards remain corporate-authored

All 11 original references exist; two relevant papers were added. No fabricated
reference was found. See validation/v121_reference_audit.json for all 13 entries.

REMAINING SUBSTANTIVE REVIEW RISKS
A. Practical magnitude. Matched checkpoint-inclusive savings are modest. All four
optimized compressed settings are slower than optimized modern lossless storage,
by 43.59-166.15% in the observed host times. Savings must justify those costs for a
specific maintenance use case. The paper now gives the necessary denominators and
complete comparison rather than promising general deployment benefit.
B. Breadth. DistilGPT2 and GPT2 are two related model depths, not two architectures.
The matched root contains four records, and the earlier root contains thirteen.
These establish complete scoped execution but not large-model or corpus-scale
behavior. The tested feature-precision contract differs from many practical caches.
C. Timing variation. Each timing cell is one observation with fixed execution order
and uncontrolled OS cache state. No latency confidence interval, stable ranking
across hosts or general speedup follows. The earlier near-tie is not a robust win.
D. Source availability. Both compressed GPT2 three-record cells replay 144 source-
stage pairs. Source-free repair is not universal; refused requests need sufficient
source access or remain unresolved. Numerical fallback and source replay differ.
E. Oracle independence. Fresh reconstruction reads retained inputs independently
of candidate state but shares the numerical solver. Actual byte comparisons cover
models, canonical state and required trust artifacts; they do not eliminate a
solver bug common to both executions. Small rational review checks narrow specific
algebra risks without changing that boundary.
F. Quality. No new quality result exists for the matched target. The earlier quality
study tolerates a small measured loss relative to its matched sequential comparator;
it does not show predictive improvement or uniform population guarantees.
G. Verification/reproduction. Proofs assume the frozen finite evaluator, correct
outward arithmetic, input provenance and stated execution environment. Hashes do
not prove semantic containment. The portable archive verifies supplied records
and arithmetic; full fresh reproduction from external inputs was not performed.
H. Submission and rights. Author eligibility, history, consent and metadata remain
unverified. Source release licensing and complete indirect anonymity of historical
public material remain unresolved. The evidence archive is author-side; TACL
prohibits external review supplements. No manuscript upload was performed.
