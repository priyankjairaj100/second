QSS: Tavory, Gade, Sarig, Touitou, Guy, 2026
Exact Unlearning via Quantized Sufficient Statistics, v1, 5 October2026.
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07197v1
Read introduction, method and guarantee-specific constructions. A frozen schema
indexes additive prediction corrections. Label and instance deletion are distinct,
and schema deletions trigger reconstruction. This is prior work for frozen-schema
exact-statistic deletion; the present target instead reconstructs adaptive weight
codes under a declared calibration constructor. No broad bitwise-novelty claim.
