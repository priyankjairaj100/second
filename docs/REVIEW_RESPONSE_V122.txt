Response to the external manuscript review, V122
29 October 2026

Decision

Substantial empirical revision is warranted before submission to TACL. The
review's strongest objections are the missing quality evidence for the headline
targets, the tiny calibration range, and the omitted source-only cold baseline.
The current results establish a scoped storage/source-access/latency tradeoff.
They do not establish a general speedup or a compelling deployment advantage.
The review's numerical ratings are subjective, not official TACL decisions.

The central contribution should remain adaptive recovery of exact weight codes
from lossy calibration intervals, followed by a canonical reusable successor
state. Generic interval arithmetic, residual bounds, decision margins and exact
subtraction are established ingredients. A stronger paper needs evidence about
when this combination is useful. Editorial changes alone cannot provide that.

This checkpoint revises the same open manuscript and adds generated correctness
checks. It does not run a new empirical study. The closed V119 campaign, its
failures and all consumed attempts remain unchanged. The new empirical plan is
docs/TACL_REVISION_EVIDENCE_PLAN_V122.txt; it is a draft, not a job registration.

Evidence-package clarification

The reviewer received Second-TACL-v121-PDF.zip, the download fallback containing
only the PDF. Their statement about that ZIP is correct. A separate full author
evidence archive already existed. The V122 full archive now also carries this
response, the revision plan, generated checks and the reference audit. Its byte
verification is not a clean empirical reproduction. TACL does not permit an
external review supplement; the evidence archive is for author-side inspection.

1. Quality for the exact headline targets: unresolved

The thirteen-record DistilGPT2 study uses N0=1664 and cannot qualify the four-record
DistilGPT2/GPT2 targets with N0=512. The existing measured quality degradation
against the custom sequential constructor is small and passes its declared guard;
it is not equality, and that comparator is not an established GPTQ implementation.
The manuscript retains this distinction. Required next evidence is quality on all
four model/retained-set strata, with identical resource-study target identities,
plus a separately defined practical GPTQ reference. No new quality value is added.

2. Operating range and independent replication: unresolved

The current two related models and two-step trajectories do not establish wider
feature-width, calibration-size or architecture coverage. Millions of compared
codes are output coverage, not independent samples; this is now explicit. The
revision plan prioritizes distinct roots and a calibration-size sweep before a
more expensive architecture extension. Unadmitted sizes must remain failures of
the admitted operating range, not silently disappear or trigger relaxed caps.

3. Minimal-storage cold comparison: design explained, baseline still missing

Source inspection confirms that the existing cold arm reconstructs retained
features and writes a lossless successor. Its equal storage is a design choice,
not evidence of minimal cold storage. Section 6 now states this. The access
conditions distinguish lossless state (neither source class required), compressed
state (retained-source replay on refusal), and hybrid Gram state (deleted-source
access for subtraction). A source-only cold state requires a separate canonical
representation, implementation, audit and experiment; it has not been measured.

4. Practical economics: arithmetic added, deployment case still unestablished

Section 6 now reports the absolute E16 storage savings and an explicit shared
installation model. For independent states with one additional codec installation
I, the net saved bytes are sum(B_lossless - B_compressed) - I. The actual I is
23,870,575 bytes. At twelve retained records, gross saving is 33,959,262 bytes,
leaving 10,088,687 bytes for one state. At six records, gross saving is 16,997,113
bytes, so one state uses 6,873,462 additional bytes after this charge. Two separate
six-record states sharing that installation save 10,123,651 bytes in total.

These are sensitivity calculations, not a measured multi-tenant deployment.
They assume separate per-state archives and no further cross-state deduplication.
The 6.89% preparation-inclusive compressed time penalty is now stated. Binary64
is the exact reference contract chosen for this study; its use is not evidence
that a deployment requires binary64. A lower-precision target needs its own
quality and correctness comparison. Prices, deletion frequencies and operational
constraints must be supplied or measured before claiming economic benefit.

5. Executable specification: substantially addressed in the manuscript

The paper now gives prepare/verify/replay/commit pseudocode, separates canonical
state from trust sidecars, and specifies outward elementary sums/products,
norm bounds, checked square-root proposals, nonfinite refusal, gradual underflow,
midpoint cells and bounded refinement. Approximate inverse/BLAS values are
proposals; directed checks authorize acceptance. Native fast-math and fused
contraction are disabled. The evaluator contract binds ordered binary64 feature
operations, MPFR endpoint agreement, source/runtime/CPU-dispatch identities and
single-threaded numerical libraries. Changed bindings require requalification
and replay must reproduce the original feature words. These descriptions were
checked against the frozen source. They are not a formal proof of the executable.

6. Independent oracle: expanded, with remaining scope explicit

V121 already contained an independently written rational checker for suffix/dual
identities, conditional quadratic minimization, ties and endpoint cells. That
checker was absent from the PDF-only download. V122 adds 486 small feature boxes
and 39,366 enumerated vertex/interior realizations. All coefficient perturbation
checks pass, along with 13,770 point-level implications of the uniform-cell
condition. Norms are bounded conservatively using rational quantities. Sampling
these finite points does not itself prove the continuum theorem.

V122 also checks elementary helpers extracted from the frozen Python reference
against exact rational values: 316 finite containment cases pass; 26 nonfinite
cases are identified as requiring refusal. Values include cancellation, signed
zero, subnormals, adjacent-to-one values and extreme magnitudes. This is not a
test of the Linux native C verifier or evidence that production transaction
aborts preserve every predecessor byte. Those checks remain in the next gate.

The bundled Python's NumPy import failed before the first check because its
Accelerate symbol was unavailable on this macOS. The successful run used existing
system Python 3.9.6 and NumPy 1.26.3. No dependency or production runtime changed.
Exact execution metadata and the failed attempt are recorded in the check report.

7. Archive precision and success: new sufficient bound, empirical curve pending

Section 4.3 now connects feature-box radius to coefficient error. With center
Xbar, entrywise radius E, epsilon=||E||_F, kappa=||Xbar||_F and positive ridge beta,
the Gram perturbation is at most gamma=2*kappa*epsilon+epsilon^2. Resolvent bounds
give D_ih=gamma/beta+||Kbar_h,S_i||_2*gamma/beta^2. Propagating D along the center's
accepted prefix gives a sufficient decision-cell condition. Cell widths determine
epsilon; small ridge and large earlier rounding residuals worsen the bound.

This standard perturbation argument strengthens the explanatory theory but is
conservative. It does not establish achievable archive precision or completion
probability. The new rational enumeration supports algebraic review only.
Changed-code counts, changed codes certified without replay, stage margins,
interval widths and precision-success curves remain unmeasured. Section 7 now
reports replay with the compressed denominator: six of eight compressed requests
use no replay, while all eight lossless requests use none. These eight compressed
requests cover four target/endpoint strata, not eight independent roots.

8. Timing and clean reproduction: unresolved

The single-observation fixed-order results remain exactly as measured. The near
0.9% timing difference is not promoted to a stable win. Repeated fresh-process
timings, randomized order and disclosed cache conditions are planned. A full
clean reproduction from external inputs and a release license remain incomplete.
The author must choose release terms; this checkpoint does not assign rights.

Other manuscript corrections

The introduction now credits verified numerical computation and adaptive robust
arithmetic. Rump (2010) and Shewchuk (1997) are verified against their primary PDFs
and independently deposited DOI metadata. The thirteen V121 reference audits are
preserved; the two new entries and all fifteen rendered entries/links are checked.
No additional hallucinated reference was found. This is not an exhaustive novelty
search. Rump is background for verified arithmetic; Shewchuk is background for
robust discrete decisions, not a same-task competitor.

Fixed N0 also changes effective regularization. If T tokens remain, the equivalent
retained-average objective has ridge lambda_l*N0/T. The manuscript now makes this
explicit instead of suggesting fixed normalization is mathematically necessary
for subtraction. Trust sidecars, canonical bytes and the grid-storage guard are
defined. A compact boxed procedure fills the explanatory role of the requested
pipeline diagram. Historical accounting is shortened; all raw history remains in
the archive. The user-requested ten content pages and no-appendix format remain.

Submission decision

Do not treat V122 as an empirically completed TACL revision. Resolve matched-target
quality and the minimal cold comparison first, then expand the admitted operating
range and measure precision success. A strong result would be useful quality plus
a clear access/storage regime in which lossless evidence is unaffordable and cold
reconstruction is unavailable or slower enough to justify certification. If that
regime does not materialize, the honest contribution is a narrow exact-repair
methodology result; more speed hunting or prose cannot establish practical value.
No conference submission, scheduler restart, cluster job or third-party message
was made in this checkpoint.
