Planning justification, not an empirical variance estimate
There are no untouched losses available to estimate variance. Assume paired
article mean-NLL standard deviation0.30 and true mean difference0 for planning.
A normal-approximation calculation for80% power at one-sidedalpha0.025 gives
ceil(((z_0.975+z_0.80)*0.30/log(1.05))^2)=297articles. Choosing320 allows modest
rounding margin. This is an assumption-based planning calculation, not a power
guarantee for the actual paired bootstrap or the maximum-article guard.
At standard deviation0.20 the analogous requirement is132; at0.40 it is528.
Thus320 does not guarantee high power under heavier variability. Retain this
limitation and report actual uncertainty; never enlarge n after inspecting losses.
The maximum guard has no population-tail guarantee from320 observations.

Inference scope
Sampling is restricted to a single bounded validation frame, and each article
uses a short prefix. Bootstrap intervals are approximate under article-level
exchangeability. They do not establish corpus-wide or deployment-wide quality,
account for unknown semantic dependence, or undo adaptive development on earlier
articles. Call this a prospectively registered untouched evaluation of a fixed
method on this admitted frame, not proof of broad model generalization.
