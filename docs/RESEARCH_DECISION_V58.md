# Decision after complete-state, quality, and GPU validation

The registered V54/V55/V56 chain is complete. It establishes bounded complete
model/state correctness, a storage--latency tradeoff, a failed pooled quality
guard, and passing observed GPU likelihood parity. It does not establish the
planned practical efficiency-and-quality claim.

## What the results identify

All four methods return the same declared fixed-anchor model. Compression is
therefore not responsible for its 5.9221% perplexity regression against matched
sequential calibration. The anchor target trades away dependence on earlier
calibrated stages, enabling source-local evidence, but its useful quality must
be demonstrated separately. The current 1.059221 ratio exceeds the frozen 1.05
tolerance. Passing the every-article guard cannot override the pooled failure.

The representation also has limited deployment value on this root. It saves
3.11--4.73% of declared deployment bytes versus cache, with slower requests.
Hybrid defeats it in both time and storage on the first request. Its second
request offers a tradeoff against cache, and improves both metrics against
hybrid. That is neither universal dominance nor a repeated performance result.

The raw-source oracle and both byte audits support correctness within the
trusted pinned implementation. GPU agreement validates the exposed likelihood
evaluator. Neither result resolves practical magnitude or target quality.

## Consequence for continuation

Do not expand automatically to another checkpoint or another corpus root.
The next-checkpoint draft remains a preparation checklist, not a registration.
Do not change V54/V55/V56, their inputs, thresholds, exclusions, or ledgers.

A new development proposal should answer a concrete scientific question before
requesting compute: can a precisely declared source-local calibration target
meet the existing useful-quality requirement while retaining an advantage over
matched strong controls? The current receipts alone do not identify a remedy.
Changing anchors, features, grids, or sequential dependencies changes the target
or access contract and must be declared, rather than described as a codec fix.

First define that target and its control/access/state obligations, inspect prior
failed candidates, and establish resource admission. Only then register bounded
development comparisons on appropriately declared data. Exposed diagnostic
articles remain development data; all sixty exclusions persist. A fresh failure
must remain reportable, without threshold adjustment or favorable-only retries.

Alternatively, a methodological paper can center the exact recovery contract
and these cost/quality limits, but that requires a credible novelty and relevance
argument. Rewording alone cannot manufacture an ACL-strength contribution.
The current draft should preserve both the correctness result and negative
practical findings. It is not submission-ready, and no acceptance is promised.

No successor experiment or resource budget is selected by this document.
