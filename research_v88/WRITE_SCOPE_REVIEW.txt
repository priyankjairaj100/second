Slurm logs: submit.py names one log per allocation. The worker's RLIMIT_FSIZE

Temporary filesystem

per active run_limited call. Its limited child inherits the descriptors and

The stream allowance and stored summaries are different scopes. The stream
bound alone says nothing about other compiler or test temporary files.

Allocation overhead and observations
Allow for per-file block rounding, directories, filesystem metadata, atomic
replacement overlap and failures. The observed allocation fragment is4096bytes
on both host filesystems. XFS allocation behavior is not a logical-file-size
identity. A reviewed allowance remains distinct from a capacity reservation or
a guarantee against concurrent writers. The guard must include all requested
bytes and inodes and never credit free space from a different filesystem.
